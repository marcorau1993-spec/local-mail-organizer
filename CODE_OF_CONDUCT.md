# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

This project is committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free
community. Be considerate, focus criticism on ideas and code, welcome different
levels of experience, and protect the privacy of everyone involved.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
doxxing, publishing private mailbox data, and sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that
violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or
permanent ban from project spaces.

When reporting conduct concerns, avoid public disclosure of personal information.
Use a private GitHub contact channel made available by the repository owner. Reports
will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where reasonably possible.
